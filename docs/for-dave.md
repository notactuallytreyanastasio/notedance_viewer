# DaveDance: running a song in class

DaveDance puts a song on the TV and hands each kid their own part on an iPad.
You press play on the TV. Every iPad follows the red line.

Address: **https://dave.bobbby.online**

The first time you open any page, a short walkthrough starts by itself.
The **Walk me through using this** button replays it whenever you want.

## Before class: add the song

1. Open the score on Noteflight Learning.
2. Copy the address from the top of the browser.
3. Go to dave.bobbby.online, paste it in the box, press **Import**.

That takes a few seconds and lands you on the TV view. The song is saved,
so next time it is just there in the list. Paste the same link again
after editing the score in Noteflight and it updates in place.

No link handy? **Load the example song** gives you Mary Had a Little Lamb
for three xylophones to try everything with.

The small **×** on a song takes it off the list. Pasting the link again
brings it back.

## On the TV

From the song list, press **Open on the TV**.

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Play / Pause** | Starts and stops the whole room. Every iPad follows this clock. |
| ⏮ | Back to the beginning. |
| **Start from measure 1 2 3 …** | Plays from that measure. The iPads jump with you. |
| 🔊 | Sound on or off. The TV plays a simple xylophone of every part. |
| **Kids join** | Puts a big QR code and the song's two-word address on the screen for the iPads. |

On a laptop keyboard: **space** plays and pauses, **←** starts over,
**m** mutes.

The song stops on its own at the end and winds back to the start.

Next to **Kids join** it says how many iPads are on the song and which
instruments they picked, so you can see when everyone is in.

## On the iPads

Every song has a two-word address, like **dave.bobbby.online/sunny-fox**.
It is printed under the song in the list and under the QR code on the TV.
Capitals and the hyphen do not matter: `sunnyfox` works too.

1. Press **Kids join** on the TV. A kid points the iPad camera at the
   code and taps the link, or types the two words into Safari. Or send
   them the **Kids' link** from the song list.
2. The kid taps their instrument. The bars are big on purpose. Every
   note shows its letter inside the notehead, in the color you gave it
   in Noteflight.
3. That is it. The red line moves when you press play on the TV. There is
   nothing for a kid to press.

The iPad remembers the instrument, so the same kid gets the same part
next time. **Change instrument** at the top swaps it. The small **?** in
the corner replays the kids' walkthrough.

Tip: on an iPad, Share → **Add to Home Screen** gives DaveDance its own
icon, so kids can open it like an app.

## If something looks off

- The dot next to the status is green when the iPad is connected and
  red while the song plays. Grey means it has not connected yet: check
  the wifi, then pull down to refresh.
- A kid on the wrong note? Have them refresh the page. It jumps straight
  to wherever the song is.
- No sound from the TV? Press pause, then play. Browsers only allow
  sound after you have pressed something.
- Import says it could not read the link? It needs the full
  `…learning.noteflight.com/scores/view/…` address of a score page, not
  a folder or a search.
- Import says Noteflight did not hand over the score? In Noteflight,
  share the score with anyone who has the link, then paste it again.
- Import says the score uses something DaveDance cannot read yet? Send
  Bobby the link. The reader is strict on purpose and learns one real
  score at a time.
- Import says the song has a repeat, a segno or a coda? The line and
  the clock follow them: Twinkle plays through twice, Nine to Five goes
  back to the segno after bar 30, jumps to the coda after bar 12 and
  plays the coda's repeat. A bar that comes round again has a second
  "Start from measure" button with a small 2 in its corner.
- A kid's strip says "Reconnecting to the TV"? The iPad lost wifi. It
  catches up by itself the moment it is back.
